## Territory
NUTS III Douro – 19 municipalities. Xisto walls, socalcos, quintas, adegas.

## Ten species + architecture link
1. **Pinheiro-bravo (*Pinus pinaster*)** – 51.6% DOURO forest – beams, flooring, windbreak
2. **Oliveira (*Olea europaea*)** – mortórios/socalcos – terraces, lagares, courtyard shade
3. **Amendoeira (*Prunus dulcis*)** – Douro Superior – terraces, deciduous shade
4. **Eucalipto-comum (*Eucalyptus globulus*)** – 3.8% – only formwork/posts, fire risk
5. **Sobreiro (*Quercus suber*)** – Baixo/Cima Corgo – cork insulation
6. **Azinheira (*Quercus rotundifolia*)** – 2.5% – lintels/beams, evergreen shade
7. **Carvalhos – negral (*Q. pyrenaica*) + cerquinho (*Q. faginea*)** – 2.1% – best structural wood, winter gain/summer shade
8. **Castanheiro (*Castanea sativa*)** – cool slopes – frames, furniture, cellars
9. **Zimbro (*Juniperus oxycedrus*)** – Lusitano-Duriense endemic – posts, windbreak hedges
10. **Amieiro (*Alnus glutinosa*) + lodão (*Celtis australis*)** – riparian – pergolas, evaporative cooling
