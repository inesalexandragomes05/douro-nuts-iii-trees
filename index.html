Douro NUTS III – Ten Common Trees

Environment, Comfort and Energy Efficiency — NUTS III Douro, Portugal / inesalexandragomes05

Ten Species

01 –
Pinheiro-bravo — Pinus pinaster51.6% of DOURO forest (IFN6). Most extensive.Architecture: beams, flooring, frames, formwork for xisto houses. Evergreen windbreak, permanent shade – avoid south of openings.
02 –
Oliveira — Olea europaeaMortórios, socalcos, olive groves (ADVID).Architecture: stone terraces, lagares, oil stores; high-calorific firewood; evergreen courtyard shade.
03 –
Amendoeira — Prunus dulcisDouro Superior cultural landscape (ADVID).Architecture: terraces, caseiro houses; deciduous – summer shade, winter sun.
04 –
Eucalipto-comum — Eucalyptus globulus3.8% DOURO forest (IFN6).Architecture: only formwork, posts, firewood – shrinkage, fire risk. Keep away from housing.
05 –
Sobreiro — Quercus suberBaixo Corgo / Cima Corgo, mixed with zimbro (ADVID).Architecture: cork for thermal-acoustic insulation of roofs/walls – key for energy efficiency; rustic floors.
06 –
Azinheira — Quercus rotundifolia2.5% DOURO forest, Cima Corgo (IFN6/ADVID).Architecture: lintels, beams, firewood/charcoal; farmhouse-montado system. Evergreen shade in arid sites.
07 –
Carvalhos — negral Quercus pyrenaica + cerquinho Quercus fagineaCarvalhos 2.1% IFN6; carvalho-duriense (ADVID); Trás-os-Montes on xisto/granite (JBUTAD).Architecture: best local structural wood: beams, stairs, doors, windows, barrel staves. Deciduous – summer shade, winter gain.
08 –
Castanheiro — Castanea sativaCool slopes, water lines (ADVID/IFN6).Architecture: frames, structure, furniture, cellars; durable without treatment.
09 –
Zimbro — Juniperus oxycedrusLusitano-Duriense endemic, Douro valley east of Tua (JBUTAD/ICNF).Architecture: posts, vineyard poles, fences; low windbreak hedge without blocking ventilation/view.
10 –
Amieiro — Alnus glutinosa + lodão Celtis australisRiparian galleries: amieiros, lodão, áceres (ADVID).Architecture: pergolas/latadas over patios; evaporative cooling, humidity regulation by water lines.
