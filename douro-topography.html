The Marão massif rises to 1,416 m and Montemuro to ~1,381 m in the west, while the Douro river falls to ~40–80 m at Régua / Foz Côa. This enclosed valley topography drives hydrology (tributaries drain toward the Douro) and climate (mountains block Atlantic air — summer aridity, winter cold-air pooling on valley floors).
